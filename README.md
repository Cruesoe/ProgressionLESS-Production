# ProgressionLESS: Production

**All the Progression goodness, less research.**

Overhauls production research, so workstations unlock in their proper tech era.

The "LESS" is less research: Progression: Production adds up to 31 research projects depending on your mods. This fork cuts that to at most 10, grouping related workstations into projects such as Workbenches, Industrial workshops and Chemfuel appliances, and moving the rest back to the research they came from, such as Smithing, Electricity, gas extraction and Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Production's large workstations. Everything stays in the same era and at the same stage within it, and still needs the other research it did before.

Forked from [Progression: Production](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3453408412) by ferny.
